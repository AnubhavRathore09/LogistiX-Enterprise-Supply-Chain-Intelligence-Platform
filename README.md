# 📌 Project Overview

Modern supply chain organizations generate massive amounts of operational data every day. However, raw data alone provides little business value without proper analysis and visualization.

**LogistiX** is an end-to-end Supply Chain Intelligence Platform developed to transform raw operational data into meaningful business insights using industry-standard analytics tools.

The project demonstrates the complete data analytics lifecycle—from raw data processing to executive dashboards—following practices commonly used by professional Business Intelligence and Data Analytics teams.

Instead of focusing only on data visualization, this project emphasizes business understanding, KPI development, analytical thinking, and actionable recommendations that support strategic decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Large organizations often struggle with:

- Limited visibility into overall business performance
- Delayed identification of supply chain bottlenecks
- Inefficient inventory and logistics planning
- High late-delivery risk
- Lack of centralized executive reporting
- Difficulty monitoring customer purchasing behaviour
- Slow business decision-making due to fragmented data

This project addresses these challenges by developing a centralized Business Intelligence platform capable of analysing operational, customer, logistics, and sales data.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Build a complete end-to-end Business Intelligence solution
- Perform extensive data cleaning and preprocessing
- Analyse sales, customers, logistics, and operational performance
- Develop executive-level KPIs
- Design interactive Tableau dashboards
- Solve real-world business questions using SQL
- Generate strategic recommendations backed by data
- Demonstrate industry-ready Data Analyst skills

## Prerequisites

Ensure the following software is installed on your system:

- Python 3.11+
- MySQL 8.0+
- Tableau Public / Tableau Desktop
- Microsoft Excel 365

# 📌 Key Business Insights

The analysis uncovered several valuable business insights:

### 💰 Sales Performance

- The business generated more than **£36 Million** in total sales.
- Revenue remained relatively stable throughout the year with seasonal fluctuations.
- Europe emerged as one of the strongest-performing markets.

---

### 👥 Customer Insights

- Consumer customers generated the largest share of total revenue.
- Corporate customers maintained consistent purchasing behaviour.
- A relatively small number of countries contributed a significant portion of overall sales.

---

### 🚚 Supply Chain Insights

- Late deliveries continue to represent an operational challenge.
- Standard Class shipping recorded the longest average delivery time.
- Delivery performance differs considerably across global markets.

---

### 📈 Business Performance

- Overall profitability remained positive.
- Several departments significantly outperformed others in revenue generation.
- KPI monitoring enables faster and more informed business decisions.

---

# 💡 Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

- Improve logistics planning to reduce late deliveries.
- Expand investment in high-performing markets.
- Strengthen customer retention strategies.
- Optimise inventory based on seasonal demand.
- Improve Standard Class shipping efficiency.
- Increase focus on high-margin product categories.
- Continue monitoring executive KPIs through interactive dashboards.
- Implement predictive analytics for demand forecasting.
