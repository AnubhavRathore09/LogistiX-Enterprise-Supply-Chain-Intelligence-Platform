# Stakeholder Analysis

## Overview

The LogistiX platform is designed to support multiple business stakeholders across the organization. Each stakeholder has different responsibilities, business goals, and analytical requirements. Understanding their needs helps design dashboards, KPIs, and reports that support effective decision-making.

---

# Primary Stakeholders

## 1. Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

### Responsibilities

- Monitor overall business performance
- Review revenue and profitability
- Track company growth
- Make strategic business decisions

### Business Goals

- Increase revenue
- Improve profitability
- Enhance customer satisfaction
- Improve operational efficiency

### KPIs Required

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Customer Growth
- Regional Performance

---

## 2. Chief Operations Officer (COO)

### Responsibilities

- Oversee supply chain operations
- Improve operational efficiency
- Monitor logistics performance

### Business Goals

- Reduce operational delays
- Improve delivery performance
- Optimize warehouse operations

### KPIs Required

- Order Cycle Time
- Delivery Delay
- On-Time Delivery Rate
- Shipping Efficiency
- Operational Performance

---

## 3. Supply Chain Manager

### Responsibilities

- Manage end-to-end supply chain operations
- Monitor inventory movement
- Improve fulfillment performance

### Business Goals

- Improve supply chain visibility
- Reduce bottlenecks
- Improve fulfillment efficiency

### KPIs Required

- Order Processing Time
- Inventory Health
- Fulfillment Rate
- Supply Chain Efficiency

---

## 4. Logistics Manager

### Responsibilities

- Monitor transportation and shipping
- Improve logistics performance

### Business Goals

- Reduce shipping delays
- Optimize shipping costs
- Improve carrier performance

### KPIs Required

- Shipping Time
- Shipping Cost
- Delivery Delay
- Carrier Performance

---

## 5. Warehouse Manager

### Responsibilities

- Manage warehouse operations
- Improve order processing

### Business Goals

- Faster order fulfillment
- Reduce warehouse delays
- Improve inventory handling

### KPIs Required

- Orders Processed
- Processing Time
- Inventory Availability
- Warehouse Efficiency

---

## 6. Sales Manager

### Responsibilities

- Monitor sales performance
- Analyze customer demand

### Business Goals

- Increase sales
- Improve product performance
- Grow customer base

### KPIs Required

- Revenue
- Sales Growth
- Average Order Value
- Product Performance

---

## 7. Regional Manager

### Responsibilities

- Monitor regional operations
- Compare regional performance

### Business Goals

- Improve underperforming regions
- Increase regional profitability

### KPIs Required

- Regional Revenue
- Regional Profit
- Delivery Performance
- Customer Satisfaction

# Business Value

A stakeholder-driven analytics platform ensures that every dashboard, KPI, and report is aligned with business objectives and supports informed decision-making across the organization.
