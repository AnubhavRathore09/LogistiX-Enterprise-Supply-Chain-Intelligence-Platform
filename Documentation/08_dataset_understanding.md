# Dataset Understanding

## Overview

The LogistiX project uses the **DataCo Smart Supply Chain Dataset**, a real-world public dataset that simulates supply chain operations for a global retail and e-commerce organization.

The dataset contains transactional, customer, product, logistics, shipping, and geographical information that can be used to analyze business performance across multiple operational areas.

The primary objective of this phase is to understand the business meaning of the available data before performing any cleaning, analysis, or visualization.

---
