# Project Charter

## Project Name

**LogistiX – Enterprise Supply Chain Intelligence Platform**

---

# Project Overview

LogistiX is an end-to-end Supply Chain Intelligence Platform designed to transform raw operational data into meaningful business insights. The project focuses on analyzing supply chain operations, logistics performance, order fulfillment, customer behavior, product performance, profitability, and regional operations using real-world enterprise data.

The platform combines Python, SQL, Excel, and Tableau to build a complete analytics workflow that supports strategic and operational decision-making.

---

# Industry

Retail & E-Commerce

# Project Type

End-to-End Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Project

---

# Problem Statement

Modern retail and e-commerce organizations generate massive amounts of operational data across customers, products, orders, shipping, logistics, and regional operations. However, decision-makers often lack a centralized analytics platform that provides clear visibility into operational performance, delivery efficiency, profitability, and supply chain bottlenecks.

Without actionable insights, organizations face challenges such as delayed deliveries, inefficient logistics, poor inventory visibility, declining profitability, and inconsistent customer experiences.

This project aims to solve these challenges by building an enterprise-grade Supply Chain Intelligence Platform that converts raw business data into actionable insights for executives and operational teams.

---

# Project Goal

Develop a centralized analytics platform that enables business leaders to monitor supply chain performance, optimize logistics operations, improve delivery efficiency, increase profitability, and support data-driven decision-making through interactive dashboards and advanced analytics.
