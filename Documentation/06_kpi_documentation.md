# KPI Documentation

## Overview

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are measurable metrics used to evaluate business performance across different functions of the supply chain. These KPIs help executives and operational teams monitor performance, identify inefficiencies, and make data-driven decisions.

This document defines the KPIs used throughout the LogistiX Supply Chain Intelligence Platform.

---

# Financial KPIs

## 1. Total Revenue

**Definition**

The total sales revenue generated from all completed orders.

**Business Purpose**

Measures the company's overall sales performance.

**Primary Stakeholders**

- CEO
- Sales Manager
- Finance Team

**Dashboard**

Executive Dashboard

---

## 2. Total Profit

**Definition**

The total profit earned after subtracting costs from sales.

**Business Purpose**

Measures overall business profitability.

**Primary Stakeholders**

- CEO
- Finance Team

**Dashboard**

Executive Dashboard

---

## 3. Profit Margin (%)

**Definition**

Percentage of profit generated from total revenue.

**Business Purpose**

Measures how efficiently the company converts revenue into profit.

**Primary Stakeholders**

- CEO
- Finance Team

---

## 4. Average Order Value (AOV)

**Definition**

Average revenue generated per customer order.

**Business Purpose**

Measures customer spending behavior.

---

# Customer KPIs

## 5. Total Customers

**Definition**

Total number of unique customers.

**Business Purpose**

Measures customer base size.

---

## 6. Repeat Customer Rate

**Definition**

Percentage of customers who placed more than one order.

**Business Purpose**

Measures customer loyalty.

---

## 7. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

**Definition**

Estimated revenue generated by a customer throughout their relationship with the business.

**Business Purpose**

Identifies high-value customers.

---

# Order KPIs

## 8. Total Orders

**Definition**

Total number of customer orders.

**Business Purpose**

Measures business demand.

---

## 9. Average Order Processing Time

**Definition**

Average time taken to process customer orders.

**Business Purpose**

Measures operational efficiency.

---

## 10. Order Fulfillment Rate

**Definition**

Percentage of orders successfully fulfilled.

**Business Purpose**

Evaluates operational effectiveness.

---

# Shipping & Logistics KPIs

## 11. Average Shipping Time

**Definition**

Average duration between shipment and delivery.

**Business Purpose**

Measures logistics efficiency.

---

## 12. Delivery Delay

**Definition**

Difference between expected and actual delivery dates.

**Business Purpose**

Identifies delivery performance issues.

---

## 13. On-Time Delivery Rate

**Definition**

Percentage of orders delivered on or before the expected delivery date.

**Business Purpose**

Measures delivery reliability.

---

## 14. Shipping Efficiency Score

**Definition**

Performance score evaluating shipping effectiveness based on delivery speed and delays.

**Business Purpose**

Measures logistics performance.

---

# Product KPIs

## 15. Best Selling Products

**Definition**

Products generating the highest sales volume.

**Business Purpose**

Identifies high-demand products.

---

## 16. Most Profitable Products

**Definition**

Products generating the highest profit.

**Business Purpose**

Supports pricing and inventory decisions.

---

## 17. Category Performance

**Definition**

Performance comparison across product categories.

**Business Purpose**

Identifies high-performing product categories.

---

# Regional KPIs

## 18. Regional Revenue

**Definition**

Revenue generated by each region.

**Business Purpose**

Compares regional business performance.

---

## 19. Regional Profit

**Definition**

Profit generated by each region.

**Business Purpose**

Measures regional profitability.

---

## 20. Regional Performance Score

**Definition**

Composite score combining revenue, profit, and delivery performance.

**Business Purpose**

Ranks regional operational performance.

---
