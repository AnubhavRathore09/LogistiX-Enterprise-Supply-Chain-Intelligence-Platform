# Business Understanding

## Project Overview

LogistiX is an enterprise-grade Supply Chain Intelligence Platform designed to analyze supply chain operations and support data-driven decision-making. The project focuses on transforming raw operational data into meaningful business insights using Python, SQL, Excel, and Tableau.

The platform enables executives and operational teams to monitor business performance, identify operational bottlenecks, improve delivery efficiency, optimize profitability, and enhance customer satisfaction.

---

# What is Supply Chain?

A supply chain is the complete network involved in producing and delivering products to customers. It includes procurement, inventory management, warehousing, order processing, shipping, logistics, and final delivery.

An efficient supply chain reduces operational costs, improves delivery performance, increases customer satisfaction, and maximizes business profitability.
