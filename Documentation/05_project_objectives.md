# Project Objectives

## Primary Objective

The primary objective of the LogistiX project is to build an enterprise-grade Supply Chain Intelligence Platform that transforms raw operational data into actionable business insights. The platform will enable executives and operational teams to monitor supply chain performance, optimize logistics operations, improve delivery efficiency, increase profitability, and support strategic decision-making through data-driven analytics.

---

# Business Objectives

The project aims to achieve the following business objectives:

- Improve end-to-end supply chain visibility.
- Monitor operational performance across different business functions.
- Identify supply chain bottlenecks and inefficiencies.
- Reduce delivery delays and improve logistics performance.
- Improve customer satisfaction through better service quality.
- Increase business profitability by identifying high and low-performing products.
- Enable data-driven strategic decision-making.
- Support regional performance comparison and optimization.

---

# Analytical Objectives

The analytical objectives focus on extracting meaningful insights from business data.

The project will:

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Evaluate product and category performance.
- Measure revenue and profitability trends.
- Analyze regional sales performance.
- Evaluate logistics and shipping efficiency.
- Monitor delivery performance.
- Identify operational bottlenecks.
- Develop business KPIs for executive reporting.

---

# Technical Objectives

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow using industry-standard tools and best practices.

The technical objectives include:

- Perform data profiling and quality assessment.
- Clean and preprocess raw business data using Python.
- Engineer business-focused analytical features.
- Write business-oriented SQL queries for operational analysis.
- Build interactive dashboards in Microsoft Excel.
- Develop executive dashboards using Tableau.
- Maintain a structured and professional GitHub repository.
- Produce comprehensive project documentation.

---

# Dashboard Objectives

The dashboards should provide different levels of business insights for multiple stakeholders.

The platform will include:

- Executive Overview Dashboard
- Supply Chain Dashboard
- Logistics Dashboard
- Delivery Performance Dashboard
- Product Performance Dashboard
- Regional Performance Dashboard

Each dashboard will focus on specific business functions and support operational as well as strategic decision-making.
