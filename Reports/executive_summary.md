# Executive Overview

In today's competitive business environment, supply chain organisations generate vast amounts of operational data from customers, products, orders, logistics, and regional markets. While this data contains valuable business information, extracting actionable insights requires structured data processing, analytical modelling, and effective visualisation.

**LogistiX – Enterprise Supply Chain Intelligence Platform** is an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed to transform raw supply chain data into meaningful business insights using industry-standard analytics tools.

The project demonstrates the complete data analytics lifecycle, beginning with raw data profiling and ending with executive reporting through interactive dashboards. By combining Python, SQL, Microsoft Excel, and Tableau, the platform provides a unified environment for analysing sales performance, customer behaviour, logistics efficiency, and operational KPIs.

Rather than focusing solely on data visualisation, LogistiX emphasises business understanding, analytical thinking, KPI development, and data-driven decision-making.

---

# Business Problem

Modern supply chain organisations often struggle with fragmented operational data spread across multiple business functions. Without a centralised Business Intelligence platform, management teams face challenges in monitoring business performance, tracking logistics efficiency, identifying customer trends, and responding quickly to operational issues.

Some of the most common business challenges include:

- Limited visibility into overall business performance.
- Difficulty monitoring executive KPIs.
- High late-delivery risk across logistics operations.
- Inefficient reporting processes.
- Limited understanding of customer purchasing behaviour.
- Slow decision-making caused by disconnected datasets.

These challenges reduce operational efficiency and limit an organisation's ability to make timely, data-driven decisions.

LogistiX addresses these challenges by consolidating operational data into a single analytics platform capable of delivering executive dashboards, business insights, and strategic recommendations.

# Executive Dashboard Overview

The Business Intelligence solution consists of three executive dashboards designed to support different levels of business decision-making.

## Dashboard 1 — Executive Sales Overview

Provides a comprehensive overview of organisational sales performance through executive KPIs, monthly sales trends, market performance, category analysis, shipping mode analysis, and regional sales distribution.

---

## Dashboard 2 — Customer & Product Insights

Focuses on customer segmentation, purchasing behaviour, category profitability, geographical sales distribution, and sales-versus-profit analysis to better understand customer value and product performance.

---

## Dashboard 3 — Supply Chain & Logistics Performance

Evaluates delivery operations, shipping performance, departmental sales contribution, order status, and late delivery risk to identify operational inefficiencies within the supply chain.

# Executive KPIs

The platform monitors several key performance indicators that provide management with a quick overview of business performance.

| KPI | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Total Sales | Measures total business revenue generated. |
| Total Orders | Represents the number of unique customer orders processed. |
| Total Customers | Indicates the total customer base served. |
| Average Sales per Customer | Evaluates customer purchasing behaviour. |

These KPIs enable executives to monitor business health through a single dashboard.

# Key Business Insights

The analysis identified several important business findings:

- Sales performance varies significantly across global markets.
- Customer purchasing behaviour differs between business segments.
- Certain product categories consistently generate higher profitability.
- Delivery performance varies depending on shipping mode and market.
- Late delivery risk remains an important operational metric.
- Executive KPIs enable faster and more informed decision-making.

These insights help management identify opportunities for operational improvement and strategic business growth.

# Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

- Improve logistics planning to reduce late deliveries.
- Strengthen monitoring of executive KPIs.
- Expand investment in high-performing markets.

- Improve shipping efficiency through continuous operational monitoring.
- Continue leveraging interactive dashboards for executive reporting.
